A quadratic problem is: 
$$
\min L(x) = \frac{1}{2} x^T H x + g^Tx + c
$$

where if 
$$
H >0
$$ its a stticty convex problem (unique solution)

Because there is no constraints, we can solve the system as: 
$$
\nabla L(x) = Hx =-g
$$
We can use the cholesky factorization and compute 
$$
LL^T = \text{Cholesky}(H)
$$
and solve 
$$
L(L^Tx) = -g
$$
Given equality constraints on the form: 
$$
A^Tx = b
$$
We can then write the derivative of larange as:
$$
\nabla L(x, \lambda) = Hx + g - A \lambda
$$
We can then write up the KKT system as
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
H & -A \\
-A^T & 0
\end{bmatrix}

 \begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y 
\end{bmatrix}
 =
 -
  \begin{bmatrix}
g \\
b 
\end{bmatrix}
$$
